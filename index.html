About

I am a structural biologist and biophysicist with over seven years of research experience on the solution structure, dynamics and interactions of proteins. I combine solution NMR, SAXS/SANS, analytical ultracentrifugation, SEC-MALS, SPR and ITC with atomistic modelling and molecular dynamics.

From 2024 to 2026 I was a Research Fellow in Prof. Teresa Carlomagno’s group at the University of Birmingham, where I studied nucleotide-dependent conformational changes in KRAS variants by NMR and ITC, and developed purification protocols for labile membrane-associated signalling proteins. My PhD at University College London, with Prof. Stephen Perkins, examined how N-linked glycosylation shapes the solution structure and function of complement Factor H and a C5-Fc nanobody.

I have published six peer-reviewed papers, two as first author (including a Journal of Biological Chemistry front cover), and have supervised six students.

Research Interests

Glycosylation and solution structure of immune proteins

How N-linked glycans influence the self-association, conformation and ligand binding of complement Factor H, Fc receptors and antibody–Fc fusions, studied by AUC, SAXS/SANS, SPR and scattering-constrained atomistic modelling.

Protein conformational states and interactions by NMR

Nucleotide-dependent conformational changes in KRAS variants and their binding partners, using multidimensional NMR, chemical shift perturbation and ITC, supported by careful control of sample and nucleotide state.

Antibody and Fc conformation in receptor recognition

How the conformational flexibility of antibodies, nanobodies and Fc regions relates to receptor binding — and how biophysical measurements can be used to test computationally designed proteins.

Reliable experimental data for computational models

Preserving units, assay context and provenance in biophysical and bioactivity measurements so that they can be compared, checked and used to evaluate predictive models.

Research Experience

2024 – 2026

Research Fellow, University of Birmingham

Henry Wellcome Building for Biomolecular NMR Spectroscopy · PI: Prof. Teresa Carlomagno
  • Lead biophysicist on an oncology programme studying nucleotide-dependent conformational changes in KRAS variants by solution NMR, including HSQC and chemical shift perturbation.
  • Transferred and verified backbone resonance assignments for GDP-bound KRAS.
  • Designed and validated an enzymatic GTP-regeneration system to maintain KRAS nucleotide state during long NMR acquisitions, verified by 1D NMR time courses.
  • Characterised KRAS protein–protein binding by isothermal titration calorimetry (KD, ΔH, ΔS).
  • Developed purification protocols for labile membrane-associated signalling proteins and produced 15N- and 13C/15N-labelled samples for NMR.
  • Supervised two MSc students in recombinant protein production and NMR data acquisition.
2018 – 2023

PhD Researcher, University College London

Structural and Molecular Biology · Supervisors: Prof. Stephen Perkins, Dr Alun Coker
  • Investigated how N-linked glycans at SCR-17/18 drive Factor H self-association and C3b binding, using AUC and protein–protein SPR.
  • Purified full-length Factor H from plasma and C-terminal fragments from Pichia; performed enzymatic deglycosylation and oligomeric-state analysis by SEC, MS and AUC.
  • Determined the solution structure of the C5-Fc nanobody by SAXS/SANS with Monte Carlo atomistic modelling, in collaboration with the Rosalind Franklin Institute, Oxford.
  • Contributed AUC, mass spectrometry, SAXS and modelling to collaborative studies of CD64, an antibody Fab fragment and β2-glycoprotein I.
  • Supervised four undergraduate and MSc students through to dissertation.
